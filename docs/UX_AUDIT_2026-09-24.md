# Quest demo clarity audit — 2026-09-24

## What the headset showed

- Fire strike warnings were mainly on the floor. A player looking ahead could miss the danger disc and the small phase label.
- The sudden-death banner explained the damage multiplier but not why drones were moving the cover. Pickup and delivery drones started within a narrow interval, filling the view together.
- The middle coolant stack could hide world-space HUD text when the player stood close to it. Several side cover pieces looked small relative to the room.
- The imported explosion effects included dark smoke materials that could render as opaque black shapes in passthrough.

## Changes

- A local player inside the warning or active fire area sees **MOVE / FIRE STRIKE ON YOU** until they leave it. The warning uses the same position and radius check as combat output.
- The sudden-death countdown says **ARENA CHANGE**. Pickups and deliveries are staggered over several seconds, with fire strikes held until the swap finishes.
- The drone hull and carried cover hold a fixed world rotation during flight. Sudden-death cover, floor, and effects capture the calibrated arena pose when fighting starts. Arena calibration is available between rounds so the world cannot rotate during a fight.
- Outer cover is spread farther apart and raised to provide more hiding area. The deck plates sit above the floor; the middle coolant stack keeps its original height after an oversized version dominated the headset view. New cover has fewer, wider shoulder-height barriers.
- The health cards sit farther left of the clock. The finger-gun muzzle burst uses an RGBA glow with transparent edges instead of the imported RGB texture with a black background. The forearm spiral limits inward swivel when the right wrist crosses the chest.
- The two imported smoke sheets used by explosion prefabs are suppressed at runtime; the fire, sparks, and hit feedback remain. The urgent HUD title and caption use a depth independent TextMesh Pro shader so nearby props cannot hide the message.

## Verification

- `ThermalBatch.RebuildCheckAndBuild` rebuilds the scene, runs the regression checks, and produces the Android APK.
- Quest 3 USB capture confirmed the old warning was hard to notice and the first revised build displayed **MOVE** during an actual hazard warning. The first capture also exposed cover hiding the text, which prompted the shader adjustment.
- The revised APK was installed on the connected Quest 3. A live hazard capture showed **MOVE / FIRE STRIKE ON YOU** in front of nearby cover, and the HUD preview verified the increased health-card spacing. Startup produced no Unity script exceptions.
- The final APK built with `Build Finished, Result: Success` and was installed on the connected Quest 3. In-headset movement is still needed to judge the forearm swivel, muzzle burst, deck height, and drone orientation during a full sudden-death transition.
- A screenshot verifies only the rendered frame. A player should still judge the warning's comfort and drone pacing while moving and looking around.
- A two-headset round remains necessary to verify that both players see the same drone choreography and each gets only their own fire warning.
